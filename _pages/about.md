I am a Ph.D. student in the [Department of Statistics](https://artsci.tamu.edu/statistics/index.html) at Texas A&M University, where I have the fortune of being advised by Dr. [Suhasini Subba Rao](https://sites.google.com/view/suhasinisubbarao/home?authuser=0) and am primarily focused on time-series analysis. 
Prior to joining Texas A&M, I was working with Dr. [Jungsoon Choi](https://sites.google.com/hanyang.ac.kr/jschoi) at Hanyang University in Seoul, Korea on spatio-temporal modeling.

My research interests mainly include
- time-series analysis,
- graphical models for time-series,
- inference and optimisation on boundary,
- nonstationary process,
- and fourier analysis on spatio-temporal data.
